# Getting help

- **Documentation:** start with the [documentation](https://ferforastieri.github.io/mira/docs/). [Jellyfin compatibility](https://ferforastieri.github.io/mira/docs/jellyfin-compatibility) covers the apps.
- **Questions:** ask in [Q&A](https://github.com/ferforastieri/mira/discussions/categories/q-a), after searching it.
- **Bugs:** use the [bug report form](https://github.com/ferforastieri/mira/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Ideas:** post or vote in [Ideas](https://github.com/ferforastieri/mira/discussions/categories/ideas), and follow the [roadmap](https://github.com/users/ferforastieri/projects/1).
- **Vulnerabilities:** follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Mira does not host, store or distribute content. Questions about where to find content, and posts with links to it or with credentials, are removed.
